Thekla

Mud Dock · Bristol Harbour · Since 1984

A converted German cargo ship moored in Bristol’s harbour, and one of the UK’s best-loved small venues. Live music below deck most nights and club nights all week: Pressure on Thursdays, Pop Confessional on Saturdays.

Parking

The Grove council car park is directly outside. It’s pay & display, or you can pay with the RingGo app. Disabled bays are available.

There’s more parking in the Mud Dock car park.

Buses

Buses stop at Queen Square and The Centre. Both are a short walk away, through or around Queen Square.

Accessibility

Because the venue is a converted cargo ship, disabled access can be difficult, but we’ll do our absolute best to accommodate you.

Reserved balcony seating

Book seats in advance on our balcony for a great view of the stage.

The balcony is down one short set of steps, so please plan accordingly if you’re a wheelchair user.

Free companion tickets

If you need a carer to come with you, their ticket is free. Just show proof of your original ticket purchase.
